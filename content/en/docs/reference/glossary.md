This glossary defines the public vocabulary used in Collab.codes documentation.

## Collab.codes

Platform for creating, running, collaborating on, governing, and evolving business software with AI.

## Creation

The product area focused on transforming business intent into application changes, screens, workflows, and maintainable product structure.

## Runtime

The environment that executes the generated customer application.

## Collaboration

The product area that brings messages, tasks, agents, mini apps, and contextual work into the customer application.

## Managed platform

The Collab.codes services around the application, including identity, LLM access, cost management, and operational support.

## Studio

The contextual creation and maintenance experience loaded near the customer application.

## Page Genome

The public model for preserving page intent, layout behavior, device category, and variation without exposing internal codes.

## Molecules

Reusable Lit 3 component contracts used to keep business interfaces adaptable.

## Master config

The composition layer that connects a client project to its master frontend, master backend, common runtime, and modules.

## Collab Aura

The opinionated master frontend used by the reference runtime.

## Collab Forge

The opinionated master backend used by the reference runtime.

## BFF

Backend-for-frontend. A backend boundary shaped around the needs of the frontend experience.

## Collab Messages

The communication and task layer for users, workflows, agents, and mini apps.

## Collab Auth

The managed authentication and authorization service used across the Collab.codes ecosystem.

## Collab LLM

The managed LLM proxy for routing, monitoring, billing, cache, rate limits, and provider flexibility.

## OpenClaw

Optional external automation integration space for research, channels, support, and agent workflows.
