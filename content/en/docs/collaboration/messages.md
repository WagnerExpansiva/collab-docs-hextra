Collab Messages is the communication layer for people, tasks, agents, and contextual work inside a customer application.

It is closer to a business workflow surface than a detached chat product.

## Why it exists

Business work often leaves the application.

A question starts in the system, moves to email or WhatsApp, becomes a task somewhere else, and loses the record, permission, screen, and decision context that made it important.

Collab Messages is designed to keep more of that work inside the application experience.

## What it can support

Collab Messages can support:

- user-to-user communication;
- task orchestration;
- AI agent participation;
- contextual support;
- mini apps;
- useful links for onboarding;
- integration with external automation spaces such as OpenClaw.

## Relationship with Runtime and Studio

Messages can appear in the runtime application and in Studio.

That matters because collaboration is useful both when people execute the business process and when they suggest changes to the application itself.
