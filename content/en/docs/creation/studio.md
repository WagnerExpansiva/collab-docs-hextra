Studio is the Collab.codes experience for creating and maintaining customer applications.

The intended model is contextual: the Studio should stay close to the application, so users and AI agents can understand the screen, record, workflow, and business intent behind a change.

## When to use

Use Studio when a team needs to:

- suggest a change directly from an application page;
- comment on a screen with business context;
- draft an AI-assisted change;
- route the change through review;
- publish the result with the right policy.

## How it works conceptually

The Studio is designed as a client-side layer loaded near the customer's application experience.

A change can begin where the user notices the issue. It can be drafted locally, reviewed, moved into version control, and then published automatically or manually depending on the customer's process.

## Governance

Contextual editing does not mean uncontrolled editing.

The workflow should respect:

- permissions;
- approvals;
- environments;
- pull requests;
- auditability;
- publishing policies.

Small companies can use a lighter path. Larger organizations can add approval layers.
