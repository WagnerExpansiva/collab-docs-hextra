Business applications need more than screens and routines.

They need operational memory: what changed, what failed, what must be observed, and how core data remains trustworthy over time.

## Audit

Auditability records important actions and decisions.

In a business runtime, audit is not just a compliance feature. It helps teams understand who changed a record, which routine produced a result, and what context existed when the change happened.

## Monitoring

Monitoring helps teams observe runtime behavior.

That includes technical health, table or process changes, operational failures, and signals that may need human attention.

## MDM

Master data management helps keep important business entities consistent.

For a client application, this can matter for customers, products, relationships, catalogs, or any domain object that becomes a source of truth.

## Why it belongs in runtime

If audit, monitoring, and MDM are added only after the application is built, they become fragile.

Collab Forge gives the backend side a place to make these concerns part of the runtime foundation.
