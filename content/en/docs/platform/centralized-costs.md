Centralized costs mean that Collab.codes can manage shared operational services, monitor usage, and charge transparently for the value of that management.

The goal is not to hide infrastructure. The goal is to make operational cost easier to understand and control.

## Where centralization helps

Centralization is useful for:

- LLM usage;
- model routing;
- identity services;
- billing;
- monitoring;
- support;
- managed platform operations.

## Why customers may prefer it

Many teams do not want to manage every provider relationship, usage dashboard, cost anomaly, model upgrade, and operational rule themselves.

Collab.codes can centralize that work while still exposing enough information for customers to understand usage and value.

## Relationship with no lock-in

Centralized cost management should be a convenience, not a trap.

Customers can benefit from managed services while still keeping a path to their own providers if they choose to leave the ecosystem.
